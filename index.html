Polo IQ — Intelligence That Wins.

Know everything about your team and opponent before the whistle blows.
Know what to do the moment it does.
Innovative statistics tracking, customized through the lens of your team's goals.

Track live by voice — say what happened and it's logged instantly with your phone's camera at the scoreboard clock to timestamp every event and stat to the real game clock automatically. Make up your own stats and notes on the fly geared towards your team's goals — "bad pass," "too high in the lane" — and they're tagged to the player and matchup. Underneath it all, the tactics engine runs on 36+ degrees of freedom per recommendation: years of matchup history, this game's live momentum, scouted notes on the exact defender you're facing, hot and cold streaks, foul trouble, shooting and sprint efficiency, lineup value comparisons, and rivalry-specific history, all cross-referenced to tell you who should be in the water, what play to run, and why — backed by real data instead of a gut call. And every player, coach, and manager gets their own page, so every stat, note, and play call lives in exactly one place.

How it works

Claim your team

Find your school on the schedule, claim it, and confirm you're really from that program.

2
Track every game live

Manage mode gives you a voice-assisted box score tracker — goals, exclusions, steals, turnovers, all logged in real time and timestamped to the real game clock if you sync it via camera. Say anything that's not a fixed stat — "bad pass," "too high in the lane" — and it's captured as a coach note instead.

3
Let the tactics engine work

Every logged event feeds back in — matchup history, hot streaks, live in-game patterns, and recurring coach notes — surfaced right when you need a lineup call.

How the recommendation engine actually works

Every tactics call is built from three sources: this game's live event log, every historical box score on record for both teams (pulled from official results across multiple seasons), and the free-form notes a coach has logged on individual players. It's not a black-box score — open the tactics board and it shows you, in plain language, exactly which of the signals below drove each suggestion.

Pattern flags

Log the same note on a player twice and the tactics engine surfaces it as a recurring flag worth accounting for — no manual review needed.

          Where your data lives

          Your roster, games, live stats, plays library, and any box scores you've pasted in — shared with everyone who opens this app, synced through the server. Each browser also keeps a local copy under localStorage for instant loading and as an offline fallback if the connection drops. Use Export below for a personal backup.

          The built-in Cal–Stanford rivalry history (the 8 logged games, scouting notes, and the fully-parsed 2023 triple-OT game) is hardcoded in the app's source code, not in localStorage. It doesn't show up here because there's no in-app editor for it yet — ask in chat and I'll add/edit games directly.

          Current-season rosters are NOT auto-populated. Add your players on the Teams page yourself, or ask me to research this year's Cal/Stanford rosters and I'll pre-fill them. The rivalry history's player names (e.g. Papanikolaou, who very likely has graduated by now) are historical — the app does not check them against your current active roster, so don't assume everyone named in a past game note is still on the team.
