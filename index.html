🤿 Built for water polo

Know everything about every player and team —
before the first whistle.

PoloAI is a live team hub and coaching brain for water polo — rosters, schedules, and a real-time box score tracker, plus a tactics engine that reads years of play-by-play history and this game's live events to tell you who's hot, who's struggled against a specific defender, and what actually tends to happen in this exact situation. Voice-tracked stats go beyond the box score too — log anything in your own words, not just what's on a fixed list.

How it works

1
Claim your team

Find your school on the schedule, claim it, and confirm you're really from that program.

2
Track every game live

Manage mode gives you a voice-assisted box score tracker — goals, exclusions, steals, turnovers, all logged in real time. Say anything that's not a fixed stat — "bad pass," "too high in the lane" — and it's captured as a coach note instead.

3
Let the tactics engine work

Every logged event feeds back in — matchup history, hot streaks, live in-game patterns, and recurring coach notes — surfaced right when you need a lineup call.

How the recommendation engine actually works

Every tactics call is built from three sources: this game's live event log, every historical box score on record for both teams (pulled from official results across multiple seasons), and the free-form notes a coach has logged on individual players. It's not a black-box score — open the tactics board and it shows you, in plain language, exactly which of the signals below drove each suggestion.

14+ signals per call
01Man-up / man-down state 02Score & quarter context 03Live momentum — runs, exclusions, turnovers 04Scouted notes on the defender you're facing 05Hot / cold streaks vs. season average 06Career history vs. this specific opponent 07Breakout-game history 08How the opponent is scoring in this game, live 09Foul trouble & fatigue flags 10Recurring coach notes — flagged once it repeats

What's inside

🏊
Team pages

Roster, coaching staff, schedule, and cumulative stats for every team on the platform.

📋
Live box score

Manage a game in real time with manual or voice-assisted stat entry — goals, exclusions, steals, blocks, turnovers.

🎯
Tactics board

Drag caps onto the pool, request a recommendation, and get a lineup call backed by real matchup history — not a guess.

📊
Matchup intelligence

Every logged box score builds career stats per player, per opponent — who's beaten whom, and how often.

🗣️
Custom coach notes

Voice tracking isn't limited to a fixed stat list — say or type anything ("bad pass," "too high in the lane") and it's tagged to the player, timestamped, and tracked alongside the box score.

🚩
Pattern flags

Log the same note on a player twice and the tactics engine surfaces it as a recurring flag worth accounting for — no manual review needed.

        Where your data lives

        Your roster, games, live stats, plays library, and any box scores you've pasted in — shared with everyone who opens this app, synced through the server. Each browser also keeps a local copy under localStorage for instant loading and as an offline fallback if the connection drops. Use Export below for a personal backup.

        The built-in Cal–Stanford rivalry history (the 8 logged games, scouting notes, and the fully-parsed 2023 triple-OT game) is hardcoded in the app's source code, not in localStorage. It doesn't show up here because there's no in-app editor for it yet — ask in chat and I'll add/edit games directly.

        Current-season rosters are NOT auto-populated. Add your players on the Teams page yourself, or ask me to research this year's Cal/Stanford rosters and I'll pre-fill them. The rivalry history's player names (e.g. Papanikolaou, who very likely has graduated by now) are historical — the app does not check them against your current active roster, so don't assume everyone named in a past game note is still on the team.
